The PAR Method

Structure behavioral answers around three parts. This tool checks your answers for all three, plus role-specific technical terms.

P — PROBLEM

The situation or challenge you faced.

A — ACTION

What you specifically did about it.

R — RESULT

The outcome, ideally with a number.
